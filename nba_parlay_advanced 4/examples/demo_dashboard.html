Method and limitations

Projections are weighted recent player production per minute, projected minutes, a shrunk defense-versus-position opponent adjustment, and 14,000 shared-game Monte Carlo samples. The current model does not have validated predictive calibration, play-by-play positional assignments, guaranteed starting lineups, confirmed injury status, or transaction-aware projected minutes. Primary positions are roster labels, not possessions defended. Only use books and game/odds timestamps confirmed for the chosen date. Ladder milestones are separate potential single bets, not an actual bundled parlay. No bets are placed.

Educational analytics only · Model estimates can be inaccurate · Never interpret a favorable matchup alone as positive expected value.
